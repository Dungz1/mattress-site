How the mattress works.

$MATTRESS is a memecoin launched on FOBO, a launchpad whose contracts enforce a rising price floor. This page documents the mechanics the token inherits from that platform, the public data endpoints, and the honest limitations.

1 · The floor mechanic

Every buy and sell routes 1.5% of volume into a floor pool held by an immutable contract. That pool always holds enough value to buy back the entire circulating supply at the floor price. The floor rises with volume and never deflates — there is no function to withdraw, pause, or redirect it.

In practice: when sell pressure hits, the floor absorbs it. Holders can always exit at the floor. The mattress is the buyer of last resort.

2 · Sniper tax

At launch FOBO applies a 90% tax on snipes (the same block as launch) that cools linearly to 2.5% over the first minute. The proceeds feed the floor, so front-running bots literally fund the mattress they are trying to jump on.

3 · Supply release

FOBO releases supply along a slow curve — ~35% of supply is sold by a $1B market cap. This prevents early supply walls from landing on holders. There is no separate team allocation, presale, or airdrop tranche on $MATTRESS.

4 · Liquidity

The pool is owned by FOBO's immutable hook: no LP tokens are minted, there is no withdraw function, and no admin key can touch it. This is enforced by the launchpad contracts, not by a promise.

5 · Public API

Machine-readable parameters are published at a stable endpoint so anyone — dashboards, agents, holders — can verify claims without trusting this page:

GET https://themattress.fun/api/stats.json

Returns: launch status, launchpad, mechanic parameters (floor share, sniper tax curve, supply curve), contract references, and live data sources. JSON, no authentication, CORS-open.

6 · Live data

The homepage reads live Ethereum data (block height, gas price) directly from public RPC endpoints in your browser. No server sits between you and the chain. The endpoints used are listed in stats.json under dataSources.

7 · Limits & honesty

$MATTRESS is a memecoin. It has no intrinsic value, no yield, no governance, and no promise of financial return. Floor mechanics reduce one class of risk (liquidity rugs) but do not make the token safe — meme markets are volatile and this can go to zero. Nothing on this site is financial advice. Verify every contract address yourself before interacting.
