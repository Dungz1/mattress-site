01 · The idea

Grandma kept cash under the mattress. FOBO put one under the chart.

Launchpads trained a generation to be each other's exit liquidity. FOBO's mechanics flip that: a slice of every trade stacks into a floor that can buy back every held token. $MATTRESS is the meme for it — because every price chart deserves a soft place to land.

The floor

Cash that only piles up

1.5% of every buy and sell puffs the mattress. It rises with volume and never deflates — the money is held by an immutable contract, not a team.

The exit

Everyone can always sell

The floor always holds enough to buy back the entire circulating supply. No bank run, no "liquidity removed", no dev rug. The mattress is the buyer of last resort.

The snipers

Bots pay for the bed

FOBO opens with a 90% sniper tax that cools to 2.5% over the first minute. Front-running bots fund the mattress on their way in. Sleep well, bots.

The supply

No early dumping

Supply releases slowly along FOBO's curve — roughly 35% sold by a $1B market cap. No founding team allocation, no presale wall waiting to land on you.
