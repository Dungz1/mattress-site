Roadmap

Four phases. No vague promises.

A memecoin roadmap should be honest: most of the value is the launch and the community that follows. Here is exactly what gets built — and what does not.

Phase 1 · Launch

The mattress drops

  • Fair launch on FOBO — no presale, no team allocation
  • Contract address published on this site and on 𝕏 first
  • Sniper tax active from block one (90% → 2.5%)
  • Public API endpoint live (stats.json)
Phase 2 · Floor

The mattress fills up

  • Floor growth published on the FOBO coin page, verifiable on-chain
  • Bubble map transparency — linked launch wallets flagged
  • Weekly floor stats posted on 𝕏
Phase 3 · Holders

The bedroom community

  • Holder meme contests (the best mattress memes win)
  • Cross-chain buy guides (ETH, Base, Solana, BTC)
  • Partner memes with other FOBO launches where it makes sense
Phase 4 · The long sleep

Keep it simple, keep it honest

  • No fake utility, no roadmap theatre
  • The floor keeps rising with volume — that is the product
  • Community decides what comes next

What we will not do: promise exchange listings we do not control, invent utility to pump the chart, or touch the floor. If the mattress is all there is, that is still more than most tokens have.
