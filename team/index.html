The team

$MATTRESS is built by a pseudonymous two-person crew: one developer, one community lead, both public on 𝕏. Like almost every memecoin, that means you are trusting people, not identities — which is exactly why the design removes the parts that require trust:

Zero team allocation. No pre-minted supply, no vesting wallets, no marketing wallet. The creators buy in on the same curve as everyone else, in public, and the bubble map on the FOBO coin page flags linked wallets automatically.

Zero admin keys over the floor. The liquidity and floor are owned by FOBO's immutable hook. There is no key, multisig, or upgrade path that can move them — including ours.

FAQ

Is $MATTRESS an investment?
No. It is a memecoin with no intrinsic value and no expectation of financial return. The floor mechanic reduces liquidity risk, not market risk. You can lose everything you put in.
What exactly is "the mattress"?
The floor pool. FOBO routes 1.5% of every trade into an immutable pool that always holds enough to buy back the entire circulating supply at the floor price. It only grows. That is the mattress — cash under the chart.
Can the team rug or pull liquidity?
No. There are no LP tokens to withdraw and no admin function to pause or drain the pool — the launchpad contracts own it permanently. This is verifiable on-chain by anyone.
When is the launch?
The exact moment is announced on 𝕏 via the launch post (a coin request to @fobodotfun). Turn on notifications — the contract address is published there first, then mirrored on this site.
How do I buy?
FOBO supports one-click buys from any chain (ETH, Base, Solana, BTC and more) with no manual bridging. After the launch post, the coin page link will be posted on 𝕏 and here.
Why does this site have an API?
So the claims here are machine-checkable. The launch and floor parameters are published as JSON at /api/stats.json, and live chain data is read from public RPCs directly in your browser. Verify, do not trust.
